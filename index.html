What I do

Four things I get called in for.

The through-line: I don't hand off between "analyst" and "engineer." I build the pipeline, the model and the report — so the answer arrives whole.

01

Loan portfolio & cohort analytics

Vintage curves that track collection rate, first-payment default, approval rate and average ticket size across new and renewal cohorts — and across the loan sequence, so you can see exactly where a book earns or leaks.

02

Credit policy & A/B testing

Designing and reading experiments on limit, score-band and affordability rules — with sample-ratio checks, covariate balance and significance testing, so a policy change is proven before it goes live, not after.

03

Business intelligence & MIS

Power BI from the semantic model up — star-schema data models, well-behaved DAX, and reports leadership and risk committees actually run on. Built for a single source of truth and self-service, so the business stops asking the analyst and starts asking the data.

04

Data pipelines & SQL

Production SQL across a warehouse (Redshift-style): restructured, documented pipelines that feed both the reporting layer and the experimentation layer from one clean, trusted source.

Business intelligence

Most analysts own one slice of the reporting stack. I build all of it: the SQL that shapes the data, the semantic model and DAX behind it, and the Power BI reports on top — so every number on the executive dashboard traces cleanly back to source, and there's one version of the truth.
